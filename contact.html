Before you reach out

A few things worth knowing.

How quickly will you respond?

Every brief is read personally by a senior team member within 1 business day. If you message on WhatsApp, usually within the hour during working hours.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We scope every engagement to fit the business — from early-stage brands to large enterprises. Tell us your goal in the form and we’ll recommend the right scope.

What happens after I submit the form?

A member of our team will review your brief and come back with a few follow-up questions or a proposed 30-minute discovery call — whichever makes more sense for your enquiry.

Can we meet in person in Nairobi?

Absolutely. We regularly meet clients at our Kusi Lane office (B3, Hindu Council of Kenya) or at your premises. For upcountry or international clients, we work fully remotely with no drop in quality.
